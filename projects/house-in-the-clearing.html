HOUSE IN THE CLEARING

House in the Clearing is conceived as a dwelling that establishes a direct relationship with the surrounding landscape. Rather than forming a compact object, the house is organized through a sequence of built volumes and open spaces, allowing the exterior to become an active part of everyday domestic life. The orthogonal arrangement of the plan structures the different uses while generating courtyards, clearings and visual connections between the interior and the wooded setting.

The project explores the transition between enclosed and open space, using the geometry of the house to frame specific fragments of landscape and create different degrees of privacy. Interior spaces extend towards patios and gardens, while the circulation between them produces a continuous sequence of changing views, light conditions and relationships with vegetation. Architecture and landscape are therefore understood as a single system, in which the house does not simply occupy the site but organizes and inhabits it.
