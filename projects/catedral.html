CATEDRAL

“The Axis”, starting from the Royal Palace, runs between the two main elements of the project —the Cathedral and the Orient Garden— connecting the palace with the southern part of the site, visually crowned by the dome of San Francisco el Grande. The proposal addresses the challenge of accommodating both a Cathedral and a Social Shelter within the same building, while also creating the “Jardín del Oeste”, a new green leisure space in the center of Madrid.

The two programs are brought together within a large prism in which the shelter surrounds the Cathedral, occupying the thickness of its walls, structured through a 10-meter bay. This configuration allows both uses to coexist within a single architectural system, while also generating new public spaces and responding to the presence of the Royal Palace and Madrid’s western cornice, which directly influence the arrangement and form of the project.
