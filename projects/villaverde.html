TRANSFORMACIÓN FÁBRICA DE CERVEZAS

The project transforms a former beer factory in Villaverde by using its existing industrial structures as the framework for a new mixed-use environment. Rather than replacing the complex, the intervention works through adaptation and reuse, preserving the identity of the silos, vaults, and large-scale structures while introducing new forms of occupation. Housing is inserted into both the square and cylindrical silos, adapting domestic layouts to their specific geometries and allowing the original structures to define new ways of inhabiting them.

Alongside the residential program, the former industrial spaces are reactivated through collective and civic uses. The vaulted areas accommodate spaces linked to water and leisure, while the larger silos are transformed into public programs such as a library and shared facilities. The proposal establishes a dialogue between industrial heritage and contemporary occupation, turning an obsolete productive complex into a new piece of the city where housing, public space, and collective life coexist.
